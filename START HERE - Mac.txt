PERMANOTES ON MAC - START HERE
==============================

INSTALL (the easy way - no building needed)
1. Go to https://github.com/stevenmelin/permanotes/releases
2. Under the newest release, click "PermaNotes-x.x.x-Mac.dmg" to download it.
3. Double-click the DMG, then drag PermaNotes onto the Applications folder.
4. The first time only: open Applications, then double-click PermaNotes.
   macOS will say it can't check it (the app isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate).
   Click "Done", then open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down,
   and click "Open Anyway" next to PermaNotes. Confirm with your password.
   After that it opens normally.

FIRST TIME
- To sync with your PC and iPhone: follow "SETUP SYNC.txt".
- To open a .pn file: double-click it, or drag it onto PermaNotes.

BUILDING IT YOURSELF (only if you ever want to - GitHub does this for you)
1. Install Node.js (LTS) from https://nodejs.org
2. Open the "mac" folder and double-click "Build Mac App.command".
   (If macOS blocks it: right-click it > Open > Open.)
3. Then double-click "Build Mac DMG.command".
4. The DMG appears in the "release" folder.
