EXPORT YOUR ONENOTE NOTES (Windows PC) - no Microsoft cloud needed
===================================================================

1. Open OneNote on your PC and make sure all your notebooks show up
   in the list on the left. Click through each one once so it's loaded.
   (Any notebook not in that list will NOT be exported.)

2. If any section has a password, click it and unlock it first.

3. Unzip this folder, then double-click "Export My OneNote.bat".
   - If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click
     "More info" > "Run anyway".
   - A progress bar shows how far along it is.

4. When it says DONE, look on your Desktop for:
      OneNote Export.zip

5. Upload "OneNote Export.zip" to Claude.
   If it's too large to upload, tell Claude the size and you'll get
   another way to send it.

Nothing in OneNote is changed or deleted. This only reads your notes.
