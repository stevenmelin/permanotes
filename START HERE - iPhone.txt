PERMANOTES ON IPHONE - START HERE
=================================

1. On your iPhone, open Safari and go to:
      https://stevenmelin.github.io/permanotes/
2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then "Add to Home Screen", then "Add".
3. Open PermaNotes from your Home Screen (not from Safari) from now on.
4. Tap the gear, and enter the same Supabase details and the same email and
   password you used on your computer (see "SETUP SYNC.txt"). Tap "Sign in".
   Your notes download automatically.

TIPS
- It works offline. Changes sync when you're back online.
- "Save .pn" opens the iPhone share sheet. Pick "Save to Files" to keep the file.
- To open a .pn file: Import > Open .pn file.
- If you ever see an old version, close PermaNotes fully (swipe it away) and reopen it.
