PERMANOTES
==========
Your notes on Windows, Mac and iPhone, kept in sync. Every device also keeps
its own full copy, so you can keep working if the internet or the sync
service goes down.

THE BASICS
- Left: All notes, your folders, your tags, and Trash.
- Middle: the notes in what you picked, plus Search.
- Right: the note itself. It saves automatically as you type.
- Folder "..." button: new subfolder, rename, save folder as .pn, delete.
- Toolbar: bold, headings, lists, checklists, tables, links, images,
  and the paperclip for any file or audio recording.
- You can also paste or drag images and files straight into a note.

.PN FILES (your notes as files you own)
- "Save .pn" (above a note) saves that one note, with its images and
  attachments, as a single .pn file.
- "Save all .pn" (top bar) saves EVERYTHING as one .pn file.
- Folder "..." > "Save folder as .pn" saves one folder.
- Double-click any .pn file (or drag it onto PermaNotes, or use
  Import > Open .pn file) to open it. Opening never overwrites a newer
  copy of a note you already have.
- Automatic backups: on PC and Mac, PermaNotes keeps a full-library .pn
  for each day in Documents\PermaNotes Backups, refreshed every 15 minutes
  when anything changed. The last 14 days are kept.

SYNC
- Settings (gear) > follow "SETUP SYNC.txt" once. After that, every change
  shows up on your other devices within a few seconds.
- The top-bar badge shows the status. If it says "Sync problem", open
  Settings to see exactly what failed.
- If the same note is changed on two devices while both are offline, you
  get both versions: the other one is saved as "(conflict copy)".

IMPORTING FROM ONENOTE
1. On your PC, run "Export My OneNote.bat" (in the onenote-exporter
   folder). It makes "OneNote Export.zip" on your Desktop.
2. In PermaNotes: Import > From OneNote... > choose that zip.
3. Notebooks and sections become folders. Every page is tagged "onenote".
   Anything that could not come across is listed at the end. Nothing is
   skipped without telling you.
