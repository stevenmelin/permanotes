SET UP SYNC (one time, about 10 minutes)
========================================
PermaNotes syncs through Supabase, a free online database you own.

A. CREATE THE DATABASE (on your computer, in a web browser)
1. Go to https://supabase.com and click "Start your project". Sign up (free).
2. Click "New project". Name it "PermaNotes", make up a database password
   (save it somewhere - you won't need it in PermaNotes), pick the region
   closest to you (East US), and click "Create new project". Wait ~2 minutes.
3. In the left menu click "SQL Editor", then "New query".
4. Open the file "supabase/schema.sql" (from this project) in Notepad or TextEdit,
   copy ALL of it, paste it into Supabase, and click "Run".
   It should say "Success. No rows returned".
5. In the left menu click "Authentication", then "Sign In / Providers", then "Email".
   Turn OFF "Confirm email" and click Save. (Only you will use this database.)
6. In the left menu click "Project Settings", then "API" (or "API Keys").
   Keep this page open - you need two things from it:
     - Project URL           (looks like https://abcdefgh.supabase.co)
     - anon public key       (a long code starting with eyJ...)
       or "publishable" key  (starts with sb_publishable_)

B. CONNECT EACH DEVICE (PC, Mac, iPhone)
1. Open PermaNotes and click the gear (Settings).
2. Paste the Project URL and the key. Click "Save connection".
3. First device only: type your email and a password, click "Create account".
   Every other device: same email and password, click "Sign in".
4. The badge at the top changes to "Synced".

Good to know
- Supabase's free plan includes 500 MB of notes and 1 GB of images/attachments.
  Each single attachment can be up to 50 MB.
- Free Supabase projects pause after a week with no use at all. If sync stops,
  log in at supabase.com and click "Restore project". Your notes are never lost:
  every device keeps a full copy, and PC/Mac also make a daily .pn backup.
